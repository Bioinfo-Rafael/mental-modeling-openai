You analyze recorded Light Aircraft Game (LAG) engagements between missile-equipped F-16s.
Team A: A0100, A0200. Team B: B0100, B0200.

# TASK

Detect a change in A0100/A0200's movement pattern or a new missile-firing action.
Other entities provide context; their behavior or changing tactical circumstances
alone do not qualify.

Return YES when the window supports either:
- A distinct before/after movement transition: e.g. straight flight to turning,
  a turn ending or reversing, or a change in climbing, descending, or speed trend.
- New firing by an A aircraft, even without a movement change, supported by
  valid ammunition decreases or missile records attributed through launcher_id.

Judge movement from command history AND actual motion across several nearby
steps, allowing command-to-motion delay. Use heading/course, bank, altitude,
vertical velocity, and airspeed as relevant. Command switches or larger
oscillations alone are insufficient: offsets -15, +30, -15 can still accompany
straight flight. Commands are targets, not displacements to add together.
Alternating corrections can also occur during a sustained turn.

Look for a change of pattern, not its size: an ongoing large turn is continuation.
Compare local segments, not just window endpoints, which can hide a turn followed
by a reversal. A developing motion trend can support detection before a maneuver
finishes; an isolated last-step command without supporting motion cannot.
Return NO when neither a transition nor new firing is supported, including
unchanged behavior, corrective oscillation, or insufficient evidence.

# DATA DEFINITIONS

State order for aircraft and missiles:
[lon_deg, lat_deg, alt_m, roll_deg, pitch_deg, yaw_deg,
 tas_mps, vn_mps, ve_mps, vu_mps]
Altitude is above mean sea level; vn/ve/vu are north/east/up velocity.
Heading is clockwise from north; handle angular wraparound. Heading and course
may differ. Latitude/longitude differences are angles, not meter distances.
Missile speed and velocity are unrecorded.

Aircraft action order: [altitude_action, heading_action, speed_action].
IDs and approximate commanded offsets:
- Altitude: 0=up (+100 m), 1=hold, 2=down (-100 m).
- Heading: 0=left30, 1=left15, 2=hold, 3=right15, 4=right30 (degrees).
- Speed: 0=up (+17 m/s), 1=hold, 2=down (-17 m/s).
These targets are executed by a low-level controller. Firing commands, Team B
movement actions, and missile control actions are not supplied.

null means unavailable, not zero; do not impute missing states, actions, or
ammunition. state_valid=0 is invalid; alive=0 is inactive, while null alive
does not establish death.

launcher_id identifies the launcher, not the target. Ammunition decreases
support expenditure but not its exact time or outcome. Missile appearance may
follow launch or reflect an already existing missile; update/remove are recording
events, and removal does not prove a hit. Do not invent targets, hits, aircraft
awareness, or coordination; recorded telemetry is not necessarily policy knowledge.

# WINDOW AND EVENT TIMING

Analyze the entire supplied window independently. Steps are 0.2 seconds apart;
state s_k precedes action a_k acting toward k+1. The final step is the current
time, and its action's outcome is unavailable. Use no observations beyond it.
A window boundary is not evidence of an onset; pre-window events are context.
An event inside overlapping windows may qualify in each independently.

Locate the earliest supported onset, not the largest command or maneuver peak.
Later observations within the window may support an earlier onset. If exact
launch timing is unknown, use the earliest supported evidence step and state
that limitation. Reasons should identify the before/after behavior or firing
evidence; inferred tactical purpose cannot replace this evidence.
