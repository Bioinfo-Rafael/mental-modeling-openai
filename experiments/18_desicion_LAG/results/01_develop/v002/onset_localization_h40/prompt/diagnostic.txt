Apply the system's detection and timing rules. Return JSON only:
- decision: YES or NO.
- events: for YES, all distinct supported events; for NO, an empty list.
- reason: a brief explanation of the decision in English.

Each event contains aircraft_id (A0100 or A0200), step, evidence_steps
(a nonempty list), and reason (one or two English sentences).
All steps must lie inside the supplied window. For movement, state what this
aircraft was doing before step c, what changed from c, and which later motion
supports that onset; return c as step. Include evidence from before c and at
or after c, with verified aircraft IDs. For firing, give the recorded evidence
and timing limitation. Keep distinct aircraft or onsets separate; combine
simultaneous turning and firing by the same aircraft at the same onset.

List accepted events only. Continuation, missile removal without new firing,
and rejected candidates belong in the overall reason, never in events.
Set decision from the accepted list: nonempty means YES; empty means NO.
The overall reason must agree. If a reason says no new event is supported,
do not return YES or populate events with non-events. Check both A aircraft
before finalizing; do not remove a supported onset just because its motion
is still developing or its exact timing is uncertain.
