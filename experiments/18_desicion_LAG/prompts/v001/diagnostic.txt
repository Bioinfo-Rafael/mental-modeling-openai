Apply the system's detection and timing rules. Return JSON only:
- decision: YES or NO.
- events: for YES, all distinct supported events; for NO, an empty list.
- reason: a brief explanation of the decision in English.

(a nonempty list), and reason (one or two English sentences).

movement with supporting actions and motion, or the firing evidence.
Evidence steps should support that description; mention uncertain launch timing
when applicable. Combine turning and firing at the same onset for the same
aircraft into one event; keep distinct aircraft or onsets separate.
